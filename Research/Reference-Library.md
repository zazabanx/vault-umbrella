- Payoff types are inferred from the title and frames, not watched.

- TikTok `playCount` is rounded by TikTok, and TikTok dates are UTC. Per-video TikTok stats from profiles and Twitch clip lengths are unavailable.
- The Burnt Peanut and bluscr3n are not in any rating round.
